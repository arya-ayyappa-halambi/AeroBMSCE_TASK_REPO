AEROSPACE INNOVATION AT PROPEL LAB–3

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling, and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab–3. Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft, drones, and autonomous systems.

Since inception, our members have completed 75+ projects, published 50+ research papers and technical reports, and represented India across global aerospace competitions including the SAE International Aero Design in Fort Worth, Texas.

WHAT WE DO
  • • Competitive aircraft & UAV design
  • • FPV drone racing & autonomy
  • • Industry-grade carbon composite layups
WHY IT MATTERS
  • • Engineers trained on real flight constraints
  • • Real hardware testing before graduation

THE FOUR SQUADRON TEAMS

Specialized competitive divisions representing BMSCE at international aerospace design competitions and national drone championships.

[SQUADRON 01]

TEAM YAKSHA

INTERNATIONAL COMPETITIONS

Our flagship heavy-lift aircraft division competing at the prestigious SAE International Aero Design series in the USA. Focuses on high-lift Selig airfoils, structural FEA, and maximum payload fractions.

KEY RECORD: 2nd Worldwide (MATLAB Award), Fort Worth, Texas, USA.
[SQUADRON 02]

BLUE IMPULSE

AEROMODELLING CHAMPIONS

Dedicated to classical and RC aerobatic aeromodelling, spot landings, and payload drop precision. Masters of contest balsa laser-cutting, carbon fiber tube joiners, and aerodynamic trimming.

KEY RECORD: 1st Place Overall Champions at SAE-India (₹1 Lakh prize).
[SQUADRON 03]

FLYING PHANTOMS

AUTONOMOUS DRONE SYSTEMS

Pioneering multirotor and hybrid VTOL drone architectures. Specializing in companion computers, PX4/ArduPilot stacks, optical flow, and GPS-denied autonomous waypoint navigation.

KEY RECORD: 1st Place Best Technical Presentation (SAE Drone Challenge).
[SQUADRON 04]

TEAM VECTORS

HIGH-SPEED DRONE RACING

Ultra-agile 5-inch and micro-class FPV drone racing team. Pushing electronic speed controllers (ESCs), high-kV brushless motors, low-latency video links, and lightning reaction piloting.

KEY RECORD: 1st Place at GAT & 2nd Place at NITK Drone Racing.
